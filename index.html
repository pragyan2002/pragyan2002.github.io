Who I Am

I'm a CS grad from George Mason University (class of 2025), focused on AI, machine learning, and data systems. I spent my college years building things that actually matter: automating 8-hour workflows down to 5 minutes, predicting solar panel output with ML models, and engineering data-observability pipelines at a credit union.

Since graduating, I've been shipping agentic AI projects, personal data tools, and open-source utilities, because the best way to stay sharp is to keep building. When I'm not deep in a dataset or debugging a distributed system, you'll find me at a Cars & Coffee meetup, geeking out over engineering under hoods, or in a specialty coffee shop thinking through my next project.

I'm drawn to roles at the intersection of ML, data engineering, and systems, especially where the work has real-world impact.
